Wave Gait Video

What to watch

Track the amber marker from rear to front along the left side of the HexaLoam hexapod, then rear to front along the right. The other five feet stay planted. This makes it easy to follow one leg through an entire swing and compare its motion with the faster exchanges in the tripod clip. The CAD model advances 60 mm per cycle with 22 mm of peak foot lift. These prescribed trajectories let students compare gait timing before testing a physical build.
