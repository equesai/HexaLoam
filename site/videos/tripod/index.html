Tripod Gait Video

What to watch

Watch the amber feet on the HexaLoam hexapod. The left front, right middle and left rear lift together while the other three stay planted. Half a cycle later, the two groups exchange roles. In the overlay, amber marks swing and teal marks stance. This CAD animation shows two five-second cycles with a 60 mm stride and 22 mm peak lift. The editable robot structure includes a LiDAR reference mount for agricultural scouting experiments. Physical walking performance still needs testing.
