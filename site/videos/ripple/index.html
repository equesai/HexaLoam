Ripple Gait Video

What to watch

Follow the lift-offs in order: left rear, right middle, left front, right rear, left middle, right front. One foot is already swinging when the next begins to lift. Four feet stay in stance as the chassis advances. Watch the six-foot diagram beside the CAD assembly to see the overlap. This ten-second ripple gait uses the same 60 mm stride and 22 mm peak lift as the tripod and wave examples. You can change the timing and regenerate the motion from the HexaLoam project files.

Narrated ripple gait

Read the English transcript

Can two feet swing at once? Follow the amber markers. In a ripple gait, the next foot lifts before the last one lands. Two swings overlap, leaving four feet planted. Explore the design on GitHub.
